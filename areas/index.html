Danvers and Five Nearby Towns

The business is based at 175 Andover St in Danvers, MA 01923, and dispatches crews to Danvers and to Peabody, Beverly, Middleton, Salem and Topsfield. Each town has its own page, written around that town's water, streets and local conditions.

Danvers itself has tidal water on the Danvers River, the Ipswich River on the western border and villages that include Danvers Center, Danversport, Putnamville and Tapleyville. According to the town resilience site, Danvers calls itself coastal with many properties in flood zones, which is why flooding and basement water come up so often here.

ZIP Codes We Serve
