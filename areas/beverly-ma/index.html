Beverly Neighborhoods and the Water They Deal With

Beverly is bordered by Danvers on the west and by Salem, Wenham and Manchester-by-the-Sea. Its neighborhoods include Beverly Farms, North Beverly, Montserrat, Ryal Side, Centerville and Cove, and each has different exposure. Waterfront streets near Beverly Harbor face tidal water, while hillside homes face roof, pipe and appliance leaks.

Ask the dispatcher about the type of water on your floor first. Salt water from a tide and clean water from a supply line are treated very differently, and a clear answer on the first call helps the crew bring the right equipment.

Bass River district

The city's 2023 resilience study covers the east bank of the Bass River from Elliott Street and Route 62 to Webber Avenue, lined with commercial and industrial properties.

Water supply

Beverly drinks water from the Salem Beverly Water Supply Board, whose treatment plant sits at 50 Arlington Avenue in North Beverly.

Coastal edge

The Bass River flows into the Danvers River, and Beverly Harbor sits at the mouth of that river, so tides shape flood risk.

Tides, the Bass River and What the City Studied

Beverly commissioned a Bass River District resilience study, finished in June 2023 with state Municipal Vulnerability Preparedness funding. It says sea level rise will keep making flood risk worse by driving stronger and more frequent coastal storms.

If your home or shop sits near the river, look at the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your address and ask your insurer about flood coverage. Water from a tidal surge is Category 3, so the restoration is cleanup and removal first and drying second.

Which Services Beverly Callers Need

Tidal and storm losses lead to flood damage restoration. A pipe that bursts in a Beverly Farms home is a job for emergency water removal, and once the water is out, water damage repair rebuilds walls and floors.

Beverly also has office and shop spaces. The Cummings Center, built in the former United Shoe Machinery complex, is a large campus of tech firms, medical offices and restaurants. Large old buildings have long pipe runs and big floors, so a leak on an upper level can reach several suites below. Tenants should ask the building manager who calls restoration.

Getting Around Beverly

Route 128 crosses the city east to west, Route 1A runs north and south through downtown and Route 62 heads west toward Danvers. Beverly Depot, Lynch Park and Endicott College are common reference points for a dispatcher.

Give the nearest cross street and a landmark. A downtown address near the depot reaches the crew differently from a Beverly Farms address near the shore, and that detail is how the dispatcher picks a route.

Before We Arrive: Steps for Active Water

Find the source

A pipe, a roof leak or tidal water each call for a different first move. Close the nearest valve if a pipe is the cause.

Shock risk

If the water reaches outlets or a panel, stay clear. Call the utility or 911 for any electrical concern before the restoration line.

Move belongings up

Stack boxes and electronics on tables and shelves above the waterline, and open closet doors to let air circulate.

Take photos

Record the room, the waterline and any damaged items for the insurer before cleanup starts.
