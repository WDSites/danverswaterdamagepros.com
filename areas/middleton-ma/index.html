Middleton Supplies the Water That Danvers Drinks

Middleton and Danvers share a water system run by the Danvers DPW Water Division. Most of the water comes from Middleton Pond, which is fed by Emerson Brook Reservoir and Swan Pond Reservoir, and it is treated at the Vernon C. Russell Water Treatment Plant on Lake Street in Middleton. Two wells off Route 114 near the town line add supply.

For a homeowner, that means a pressure problem or a street main break goes to the Water Division, and a leak inside the house goes to your own shutoff valve and then to the dispatch line. Knowing where the line between the two sits saves minutes.

What to Do While Water Is Still Spreading

Stop what you can

Close the valve at the fixture or the main shutoff. A burst washer hose and a failed water heater each have their own valve.

Switch off power

Turn off power to a wet room from a dry panel only, and stay away from outlets in standing water.

Save what is dry

Move rugs, books and boxes out of the wet area and keep family and pets in a dry room.

Document

Photograph wet walls and floors. Note the time the water was found and the source.
