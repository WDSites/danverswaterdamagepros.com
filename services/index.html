How Water Damage Work Is Organized

A water loss moves through stages, and each stage has its own service page. Water comes out first, the structure is dried second, wet debris is cleaned out third, and the rebuild comes last. Mold remediation sits beside those stages and applies when materials stayed damp too long.

Pick the page that matches where you are. If water is still on the floor, start with emergency water removal. If the water is gone but walls and floors are damp, read about water mitigation. If you are weeks past the loss and see growth, the mold remediation page is the right place.

Which Service Do You Need?

Where you areStart with
Water is standing or still spreadingEmergency water removal.
Storm, river or sewage water entered the homeFlood damage restoration.
The water is out but materials are still dampWater mitigation.
Wet debris, odor or sanitizing is the problemWater damage cleanup.
